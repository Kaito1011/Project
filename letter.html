Dear, Lablab

I just want to tell you something that has been on my mind. I know that sometimes, when someone gives too much attention or expresses their feelings strongly, it can feel like lovebombing. I understand if you ever think that about me, and I don’t blame you for being careful with your feelings.

But I want you to know that what I feel for you is genuine. I’m not saying these things just because everything between us is still new, and I’m not trying to rush you into anything. I simply want to be honest about how I feel.

♡

I don’t want you to feel pressured to believe me just because I say that I love you. I would rather show you through the way I treat you, the way I listen to you, and the way I continue choosing to know you more each day.

I know that words can be easy to say. That’s why I don’t want my words to be the only proof of what I feel. I want my actions, patience, and consistency to speak for me too.

So if you ever doubt whether I’m being genuine, I hope you remember that I’m not here just for the excitement of something new. I genuinely enjoy having you in my life. I love our random conversations, our laughs, our games, our flirting, and even those simple moments when we just talk about nothing.

I don't know exactly where this will take us, but I know that I want to keep getting to know you. I want to experience more moments with you, even if for now there's still distance between us.

And if one day we finally get the chance to meet, I hope we can look back at this little chapter and smile at how everything started.

Until then, I'll keep choosing to be here, to be honest with you, and to appreciate every moment we have.

I love you always, in all ways ❤️

Always, Your Lablab
Written with a little courage and a lot of love.
